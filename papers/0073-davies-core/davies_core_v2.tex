\begin{abstract}
We present a finite-dimensional technical core for interpreting the
quantum--classical boundary as a control-resource limitation: a state is
operationally ``classical'' (relative to a chosen conditional expectation)
whenever sustaining coherence is infeasible under a finite power budget.
Using battery-assisted thermal operations at temperature $T$ and energy
pinching $\Delta$, coherence is $C(\rho) = S(\rho\|\Delta[\rho])$ with
instantaneous loss rate $\dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}(\rho)$ under uncontrolled
Markovian dynamics; the imported maintenance bounds of \cite{MaintWork} (v2)
give $P_{\mathrm{extra}} \ge \kB T\,\dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}$ in their
corrected operational senses. We isolate the dynamical hinge required for
geometric scaling claims and prove two interface results for Davies
generators: a \emph{negative} result --- an active $\omega = 0$ channel
yields a witness-based rate lower bound via a KMS commutator with $S(0)$
(exact identity, Lemma~\ref{lem:zero}) --- and a \emph{corrected exact
Bohr-block identity} for the KMS Dirichlet form with modular weights
(Lemma~\ref{lem:blocks}), which replaces v1's commutator decomposition
(measurably false for $\omega \ne 0$: $30$--$40\%$ deviation at $\beta = 1$,
while the corrected form is machine-exact). A \emph{positive} sufficient
lemma gives linear envelope suppression under infrared exclusion, quasi-local
Bohr spectral tails, and thermal clustering of the reference
(Lemma~\ref{lem:envelope}); whether the Davies class supports the quadratic
envelope --- which \emph{is} exact in the local-sink quasi-free class
\cite{HCut} --- is stated as an open question with the obstruction quantified.
TFIM exact-diagonalization witness protocols ($R_{\mathrm{opt}}(\eps)$,
scaling and temperature sweeps) and state-side micro-tests (plateau of
$\dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}/C$, linear $\gamma(0)$ and $1/\beta$ scalings, with
step-size and PSD-clipping stability checks) are reported with reproducible
conventions.
\end{abstract}

maintain coherence against uncontrolled open-system dynamics under an

\section{Finite-dimensional operational core}

\subsection{Thermal state, energy pinching, and coherence}

Let $H_S = \sum_n E_n\Pi_n$ and, at temperature $T$,
$\gamma_S := e^{-\beta H_S}/\Tr(e^{-\beta H_S})$, $\beta = 1/(\kB T)$. Define
$\Delta[\rho] := \sum_n \Pi_n\rho\Pi_n$ and $C(\rho) := S(\rho\|\Delta[\rho])$
(nats), $S(\rho\|\sigma) := \Tr[\rho(\log\rho - \log\sigma)]$.

\subsection{Uncontrolled dynamics and loss rate}

For a Markovian semigroup $\rho_t = e^{t\mathcal{L}}(\rho)$ with
$\mathcal{L}(\gamma_S) = 0$, define $\dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}(\rho) :=
-\frac{d}{dt}C(\rho_t)|_{t=0}$.

\subsection{Control model and imported maintenance bounds}

\begin{theorem}[Imported, corrected form; proved in \cite{MaintWork} (v2)]
\label{thm:imported}
Under stroboscopic battery-assisted thermal operations at temperature $T$:
(a) $P_{\min}(\rho) \ge \kB T\,\sigma(\rho)$ unconditionally, with
$\sigma(\rho) \ge 0$ the entropy production rate, split as $\sigma =
\dot{F}^\Delta_{\mathrm{diag}} + \dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}$;
(b) $P_{\min}(\rho) \ge \kB T\,\dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}(\rho)$ under diagonal
contraction (automatic for Davies generators);
(c) against thermodynamically $\varepsilon$-efficient population baselines,
under population autonomy, $P(s) - P(s_{\mathrm{diag}}) \ge \kB
T\,\dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}(\rho) - \varepsilon$; in the free-baseline case
($\mathcal{L}(\Delta[\rho]) = 0$ and $\Delta[\mathcal{L}(\rho)] = 0$, e.g.\
pure dephasing), $P_{\mathrm{extra}}(\rho) = P_{\min}(\rho) \ge \kB
T\,\dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}(\rho)$ with no further assumptions.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Correction relative to v1]\label{rem:importfix}
v1 imported this as ``$P_{\mathrm{extra}}$, defined as an infimum over pairs
of strategies, satisfies $P_{\mathrm{extra}} \ge \kB
T\,\dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}$ whenever both control sets are non-empty''. That
unlinked-pairs infimum is $-\infty$ (battery-burning baselines) and the v1
statement is vacuous; see \cite{MaintWork} (v2) and its changelog. All uses
below are through Theorem~\ref{thm:imported}(a)--(c).
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[No-go from a uniform loss floor]\label{cor:nogo}
Fix $C_0 > 0$ and a nonempty family $\mathcal{F}_{C_0} \subset \{\rho :
C(\rho) \ge C_0\}$ within the scope of Theorem~\ref{thm:imported}(b) or (c).
If $g(C_0) := \inf_{\rho\in\mathcal{F}_{C_0}} \dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}(\rho) >
0$, then maintaining any $\rho \in \mathcal{F}_{C_0}$ requires power at least
$\kB T\, g(C_0)$ (total power under (b); incremental over efficient baselines
under (c)); if $P_{\max} < \kB T\, g(C_0)$, sustained maintenance is
operationally impossible on $\mathcal{F}_{C_0}$.
\end{corollary}

\section{Davies generators, KMS Dirichlet form, and envelopes}

\subsection{Bohr components, inner product, Dirichlet form}

For a single Hermitian coupling $S = S^\dagger$, define Bohr components
$S(\omega) := \sum_{E_m - E_n = \omega}\Pi_m S\Pi_n$, so $S =
\sum_\omega S(\omega)$ and $S(\omega)^\dagger = S(-\omega)$; bath rates obey
KMS: $\gamma(-\omega) = e^{-\beta\omega}\gamma(\omega)$. With $\sigma =
\gamma_S$, the KMS inner product is $\langle A, B\rangle_\sigma :=
\Tr(\sigma^{1/2}A^\dagger\sigma^{1/2}B)$, and for the Heisenberg generator
$\mathcal{L}^\dagger$ the Dirichlet form is $\mathcal{E}_\sigma(O) :=
-\mathrm{Re}\,\langle O, \mathcal{L}^\dagger(O)\rangle_\sigma$.

\subsection{Separation and envelopes (ceiling vs.\ floor)}

Fix a coupling region (site $j_0$) and let $\mathcal{A}_\eps$ be operators
supported at distance $\ge \eps$. Define
$\kappa(\eps) := \sup_{O\in\mathcal{A}_\eps}
\mathcal{E}_\sigma(O)/\normsig{O}^2$.

\begin{remark}[Ceiling annotation, inherited from \cite{Stress} (v2)]
$\kappa(\eps)$ is a \emph{ceiling}; witness lower bounds
(Proposition~\ref{prop:witness}) bound it from below, which is the correct
direction for \emph{rate-floor} (negative) conclusions. Maintenance
\emph{cost} conclusions on state families use the sector quantities of
Section~\ref{sec:interface}, not $\kappa$; the floor envelope
$\kappa_\perp$ relevant there is developed in \cite{Stress} (v2).
\end{remark}

\subsection{Pauli-string tail map}

With $\Lambda$ the sites and $\Omega_\eps := \{j : d(j, j_0) \ge \eps\}$,
define $E_{<\eps}$ as the orthogonal projection (normalized Hilbert--Schmidt)
onto Pauli strings supported in $\Lambda\setminus\Omega_\eps$, and
$\mathrm{tail}_\eps(X) := X - E_{<\eps}[X]$. Norm equivalences with
$\normsig{\cdot}$ hold at fixed $(N, \beta)$ with constants containing
$\lambda_{\min}(\sigma)^{-1/2}$ factors.

\section{Interface lemmas}

\subsection{Negative lemma: the $\omega = 0$ channel (exact; unchanged)}

\begin{lemma}[Exact $\omega = 0$ Dirichlet identity]\label{lem:zero}
If the $\omega = 0$ channel is $\mathcal{L}^\dagger_0(O) =
\gamma(0)[S(0)OS(0) - \tfrac12\{S(0)^2, O\}]$ with $S(0) := \sum_n \Pi_n S
\Pi_n$ and $\gamma(0) > 0$, then for all $O$,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{E}^{(0)}_\sigma(O) = \frac{\gamma(0)}{2}\,\normsig{[S(0), O]}^2 .
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$S(0)$ commutes with $H_S$, hence with $\sigma^{1/2}$; expanding both sides
of the KMS pairing and matching terms gives the identity (all modular weights
are $1$ at $\omega = 0$).
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Fixed-separation witness lower bound]\label{prop:witness}
If there exist $c_{\eps_0} > 0$ and $O_{\eps_0}\in\mathcal{A}_{\eps_0}$ with
$\normsig{[S(0), O_{\eps_0}]}^2/\normsig{O_{\eps_0}}^2 \ge c_{\eps_0}$, then
$\kappa(\eps_0) \ge \tfrac{\gamma(0)}{2}c_{\eps_0}$.
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Corrected exact Bohr-block identity}

\begin{lemma}[Modular-weighted Bohr-block identity (corrected)]
\label{lem:blocks}
For the single-channel Davies generator $\mathcal{L}^\dagger(O) =
\sum_\omega \gamma(\omega)[S(\omega)^\dagger O S(\omega) -
\tfrac12\{S(\omega)^\dagger S(\omega), O\}]$ and Hermitian $O$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:blocks}
\mathcal{E}_\sigma(O) \;=\; \sum_\omega \gamma(\omega)\, e^{\beta\omega/2}\,
\mathrm{Re}\!\left[\tfrac12\normsig{S(\omega)O}^2 +
\tfrac12\normsig{OS(\omega)^\dagger}^2 -
\big\langle S(\omega)O,\, OS(\omega)\big\rangle_\sigma\right].
\end{equation}
At $\omega = 0$ the bracket equals $\tfrac12\normsig{[S(0),O]}^2$, recovering
Lemma~\ref{lem:zero}.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\emph{Notation.} In \eqref{eq:blocks} the two norms involve
$S(\omega)O$ and $OS(\omega)^\dagger$, while the cross term is
$\langle S(\omega)O,\, OS(\omega)\rangle_\sigma$ --- with $S(\omega)$, not
$S(\omega)^\dagger$, in the second slot. The bracket is therefore \emph{not}
a commutator norm, and the expression does not reduce to v1's commutator
square except at $\omega = 0$ (where $S(0)^\dagger = S(0)$ and all three
terms assemble into $\tfrac12\normsig{[S(0),O]}^2$).

\begin{proof}
Fix $\omega$, write $L := S(\omega)$. Since $L$ has definite Bohr frequency,
$\sigma^{1/2}L = e^{-\beta\omega/2}L\sigma^{1/2}$ and $\sigma^{1/2}L^\dagger =
e^{+\beta\omega/2}L^\dagger\sigma^{1/2}$. Then, for Hermitian $O$,
\begin{align}
\langle O, L^\dagger O L\rangle_\sigma
&= e^{\beta\omega/2}\langle LO, OL\rangle_\sigma, \\
\langle O, L^\dagger L O\rangle_\sigma
&= e^{\beta\omega/2}\langle LO, LO\rangle_\sigma, \\
\langle O, O L^\dagger L\rangle_\sigma
&= e^{\beta\omega/2}\langle OL^\dagger, OL^\dagger\rangle_\sigma,
\end{align}
by moving one $\sigma^{1/2}$ through $L^\dagger$ (first two) or cycling $L$
through the trace (third). Assembling $-\mathrm{Re}\langle O,
\mathcal{L}^\dagger_\omega O\rangle_\sigma$ gives the $\omega$-summand of
\eqref{eq:blocks}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[v1's Lemma 2 was false]\label{rem:l2false}
v1 asserted $\mathcal{E}_\sigma(O) = \tfrac12\sum_\omega
\gamma(\omega)\normsig{[S(\omega), O]}^2$. This omits the modular weights and
mis-shapes the cross term; numerically (TFIM, $N = 8$, $\beta = 1$, random
Hermitian $O$) it deviates from the true Dirichlet form by $30$--$40\%$,
while \eqref{eq:blocks} agrees to $2\times 10^{-10}$
(Appendix~\ref{app:verification}). Everything in v1 that used only the
$\omega = 0$ case (the witness program of Section~\ref{sec:tfim}) is
unaffected.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Positive lemma: linear envelope suppression (corrected scope)}

\begin{definition}[Infrared exclusion]
The coupling satisfies IR exclusion at scale $\omega_* > 0$ if
$S(\omega) = 0$ for all $0 < |\omega| < \omega_*$ and the $\omega = 0$
channel is absent ($\gamma(0) = 0$ or $S(0) \in \mathbb{C}\mathbf{1}$).
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Linear envelope suppression (sufficient)]\label{lem:envelope}
Assume: (i) IR exclusion; (ii) quasi-local Bohr tails: there are $A, \xi > 0$,
$p \ge 0$ with $\|\mathrm{tail}_\eps(S(\omega))\|_\infty \le
A(1+\eps)^p e^{-\eps/\xi}$ for all $|\omega| \ge \omega_*$; (iii) bounded
weighted rates: $\Gamma_\beta := \sum_{|\omega|\ge\omega_*}
\gamma(\omega)e^{\beta\omega/2} < \infty$; (iv) thermal clustering across the
cut: for $O \in \mathcal{A}_\eps$ and near-supported $P$, the KMS-norm
factorization defects obey
$\big|\normsig{OP^\dagger}^2 - \normsig{OP}^2\big| \le
B e^{-\eps/\xi'}\,\|P\|_\infty^2\normsig{O}^2$ for some $B, \xi' > 0$.
Then there is $C > 0$ (explicit in $A, B, \Gamma_\beta, \|S\|_\infty$) with
\begin{equation}
\kappa(\eps) \;\le\; C\,(1+\eps)^{p}\, e^{-\eps/\min(\xi, \xi')}.
\end{equation}
All constants are fixed-$(N, \beta)$ quantities unless hypotheses
(ii)--(iv) are assumed uniformly: $\Gamma_\beta$, the norm-equivalence
factors, and the clustering constants of (iv) may all depend on size and
temperature, and no thermodynamic-limit uniformity is claimed.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:blocks}. For $O \in \mathcal{A}_\eps$ split each block
$S(\omega) = P_\omega + T_\omega$ with $P_\omega := E_{<\eps}[S(\omega)]$
(near-supported, hence commuting with $O$ and with $O^\dagger$) and
$T_\omega := \mathrm{tail}_\eps(S(\omega))$. Substituting into
\eqref{eq:blocks} and using $[P_\omega, O] = [P_\omega^\dagger, O] = 0$, every
term containing at least one $T_\omega$ is bounded via Cauchy--Schwarz by
$\|T_\omega\|_\infty(\|S\|_\infty + \|T_\omega\|_\infty)\normsig{O}^2$; the
$T$-independent residue of each block is
$\tfrac12(\normsig{OP_\omega^\dagger}^2 - \normsig{OP_\omega}^2)$, which
hypothesis (iv) bounds by $Be^{-\eps/\xi'}\|S\|_\infty^2\normsig{O}^2$
(splitting defects between $P_\omega$ and $S(\omega)$ are again $T$-linear).
Summing with weights $\gamma(\omega)e^{\beta\omega/2} \le \Gamma_\beta$ and
taking the supremum over $O$ gives the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The quadratic envelope is withdrawn as proved, and reposed]
\label{rem:quadratic}
v1 claimed $\kappa(\eps) \le C(1+\eps)^{2p}e^{-2\eps/\xi}$ (tail-squared).
Its proof used the false identity of Remark~\ref{rem:l2false}, and with the
corrected identity the splitting produces $T$-\emph{linear} cross terms and
$T$-independent thermal-clustering residues that the commutator cancellation
of v1 no longer removes. An exact ``tail-reduction'' shortcut
($\mathcal{E}_\sigma(O; \{S(\omega)\}) = \mathcal{E}_\sigma(O;
\{T_\omega\})$) is also numerically false --- measured relative defect
$\approx 5\times 10^{-3}$ at $(N, \beta, \eps) = (8, 1, 3)$
(Appendix~\ref{app:verification}) --- because the near parts $P_\omega$ do
not have definite Bohr frequency, so the pair-positivity argument that would
kill the cross terms does not apply. Whether the Davies class supports a
quadratic envelope under strengthened hypotheses is open; note that the
quadratic (amplitude-squared) exponent \emph{is} exact in the local-sink
quasi-free class, $\kappa(\eps) \propto e^{-2q(\omega_b)\eps}$ \cite{HCut},
and the distinction between correlator-mediated (linear) and
amplitude-mediated (squared) envelopes is precisely the one drawn in
\cite{RIP} (v2).
\end{remark}

\section{TFIM witness computations (exact diagonalization)}\label{sec:tfim}

\subsection{Model and conventions}

TFIM on $N$ spins, open boundaries, $H_S = -J\sum\sigma^x_i\sigma^x_{i+1} -
h\sum\sigma^z_i$, $h > J$; coupling site $j_0 = \lfloor N/2\rfloor$
(0-indexed), $S = \sigma^z_{j_0}$; thermal $\sigma = \gamma_S$ at $\beta \in
\{0.5, 1, 2\}$.

\begin{remark}[Site convention (v2 clarification)]\label{rem:convention}
For a distant probe site at separation $\eps$ we take $k = j_0 - \eps$
(equivalently, the maximum of the two ratios at $j_0 \pm \eps$; on an open
chain with even $N$, $j_0 = N/2$ sits right of center and the two sides are
\emph{not} equivalent). v1 wrote $k = j_0 \pm \eps$ without fixing the
branch; the tabulated values correspond to $k = j_0 - \eps$ and are
reproduced exactly by the distributed script
(Appendix~\ref{app:verification}).
\end{remark}

\sigma^z_k\}} R(\eps; O)$; by Lemma~\ref{lem:zero}, $\kappa(\eps) \ge

\section{State-side interface micro-tests}\label{sec:micro}

The weak-coherence families $\rho_E(\alpha) := \mathrm{diag}(p) +
\alpha\sqrt{p_m p_n}(|m\rangle\langle n| + |n\rangle\langle m|)$ (energy
basis, $\Delta[\rho(\alpha)] = \mathrm{diag}(p)$ by construction) give direct
access to $r(\rho) := \dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}(\rho)/C(\rho)$. As in v1
(unchanged; these tests use only exact semigroup steps and
Lemma~\ref{lem:zero}-type channels): for $N = 6$, $J = 1$, $h = 1.5$,
$\beta = 1$, center coupling, $r(\rho)$ exhibits a plateau
$\approx 0.157$--$0.158$ ($\{\pm\omega\}$ truncation) and
$\approx 0.160$--$0.161$ with an added $\omega = 0$ channel at
$\gamma(0) = 0.2$; in the $\omega = 0$-only regime the plateau scales
linearly in $\gamma(0)$ ($r/\gamma(0) = 0.2039$ across
$\gamma_0 \in \{0.05, 0.1, 0.4\}$) and, at fixed pair $(m, n) = (0, 1)$ and
fixed $\gamma_0$, as $1/\beta$ ($\beta\, r$ constant to $2\times 10^{-3}$
relative across $\beta \in \{0.5, 1, 2, 4\}$). Euler step-size and
PSD-clipping stability checks as in v1 (plateau stable to $10^{-6}$ across
$dt \in \{10^{-4}, 5\times 10^{-5}, 10^{-5}\}$ and clipping thresholds
$10^{-12}$--$10^{-16}$).

\section{State--observable interface (explicit hypothesis)}
\label{sec:interface}

The maintenance inequality bounds power via the state functional
$\dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}(\rho)$; $\kappa(\eps)$ is an observable-side ceiling.
The bridge is stated as an explicit, falsifiable hypothesis, unchanged from
v1: on families that are (1) weak-coherence, (2) population-nonextremal
($\Delta[\rho] \ge p_{\min}\mathbf{1}$), (3) coherence-supported in a fixed
Bohr sector set $\Omega$, and (4) within a validated Davies regime, one has
$\dot{C}_{\mathrm{loss}}(\rho) \ge (1 - \eta_\eps)\,\Gamma_\Omega\, C(\rho)$
with $\Gamma_\Omega := \min_{\omega\in\Omega}\lambda_{\min}(\Gamma(\omega))$
and $\eta_\eps \to 0$ as the coherence scale vanishes. The micro-tests of
Section~\ref{sec:micro} are direct numerical probes of this interface.

\section{Operational consequence (conditional, well-typed)}

Combining Theorem~\ref{thm:imported}(b) or (c) with the interface hypothesis:
for $\rho$ in such a family with $C(\rho) \ge C_0$,
\begin{equation}
P \;\ge\; \kB T\,(1 - \eta_\eps)\,\Gamma_\Omega\, C_0,
\end{equation}
where $P$ is total power under diagonal contraction, or incremental power
over $\varepsilon$-efficient baselines (up to $\varepsilon$) under population
autonomy.

\section{Conclusion}

We separated the corrected, imported thermodynamic maintenance bounds from
finite-dimensional dynamical interface results. In Davies dynamics, the exact
$\omega = 0$ identity yields operational witness lower bounds (reproduced
exactly under the fixed site convention), and the corrected modular-weighted
Bohr-block identity replaces v1's false commutator decomposition ---
machine-verified, and reducing to the $\omega = 0$ case where all v1
numerics lived. Envelope suppression survives as a \emph{linear} sufficient
bound with explicit hypotheses; the quadratic Davies envelope is open, while
being exactly realized in the quasi-free local-sink class \cite{HCut}. TFIM
witnesses ($N \le 12$) remain finite-size diagnostics, not
thermodynamic-limit claims.
