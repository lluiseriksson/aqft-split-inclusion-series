\begin{abstract}
We present a logically explicit operational program connecting three themes:
\emph{geometry} as suppressibility of cross-region influence, \emph{membranes}
as engineered interfaces implementing that suppressibility, and \emph{life}
as sustained maintenance of internal organization under finite resources. The
program composes: (i) a law-grade thermodynamic inequality relating
incremental maintenance power to the instantaneous loss rate of an
organization functional, imported in its corrected operational hierarchy;
(ii) a static geometric suppression layer in which cross-interface leakage
admits an envelope $f(\eps) \sim \mathrm{poly}(m\eps)e^{-m\eps}$ (with
$K_\nu(m\eps)$ as a canonical representative in massive homogeneous models);
and (iii) a dynamical hinge (the Rate Inheritance Principle, RIP) connecting
static suppression to separation-dependent effective dynamical rates. This
version distinguishes \emph{upper} and \emph{lower} rate envelopes
$\kup(\eps)$ and $\kdn(\eps)$ to avoid sign/quantifier errors --- a
distinction now vindicated by the companion series --- separates a law-grade
$\Delta$-track (energy pinching) from a conditional biology-grade
$E$-track (general conditional expectations), and adds two interface anchors:
a recoverability layer via conditional mutual information (CMI) and the
Fawzi--Renner guarantee, and a minimal Davies interface lemma showing how
correlator envelopes imply Davies-rate \emph{upper} envelopes (supporting
RIP-U microscopically in standard weak-coupling settings). A concrete
electrical testbed using membrane-embedded spin probes is proposed to measure
dephasing-rate envelopes and detect near-zero-frequency floors that create a
resource horizon. A dependency and falsification matrix makes the logical
structure audit-friendly.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and non-claims}

This manuscript is an operational program. It does not propose modified
quantum dynamics, does not derive the Born rule, and does not claim to solve
the measurement problem. The central object is an operational feasibility
boundary: maximal maintainable organization is constrained by minimal
maintenance power implied by thermodynamic bookkeeping and uncontrolled
dynamics. ``Life'' is used only in the operational sense of sustained
organization maintenance under finite resources.

\section{Two tracks: law-grade $\Delta$ vs biology-grade $E$}

\textbf{Law-grade ($\Delta$-track).} With $H_S = \sum_n E_n\Pi_n$, energy
pinching $\Delta[\rho] := \sum_n\Pi_n\rho\Pi_n$ and $C_\Delta(\rho) :=
S(\rho\|\Delta[\rho])$ give the cleanest proved statements.

\textbf{Biology-grade ($E$-track).} To encode membrane-relevant
coarse-grainings, use a conditional expectation $E$ and $C_E(\rho) :=
S(\rho\|E[\rho])$. Extending $\Delta$-track identities to general $E$ requires
a Pythagorean/sufficiency-type condition (Petz sufficiency \cite{Petz86};
approximate sufficiency/recovery \cite{JRSWW18}); \emph{all $E$-track power
statements are explicitly conditional on such an extension} --- and the
companion \cite{Clustering} (v2) shows the associated Gaussian reconstruction
is conditional reattachment, not the Petz map, a subtlety that does not affect
the CMI layer below.

\section{Operational framework}

$\gamma_S := e^{-\beta H_S}/\Tr e^{-\beta H_S}$; $S(\rho\|\sigma) :=
\Tr[\rho(\log\rho-\log\sigma)]$ (support convention); Markovian uncontrolled
$\rho_t = e^{t\mathcal{L}}(\rho)$; loss rates
$\dot{C}_{\bullet,\mathrm{loss}}(\rho) :=
-\frac{d}{dt}C_\bullet(\rho_t)|_{t=0}$. Control primitive:
battery-assisted thermal operations at temperature $T$, with work accounted
as battery free-energy \emph{consumption} (sign convention of \cite{Work}
v2; v1 wrote ``increase'', the sign later corrected there).

\section{Imported core law: the corrected maintenance hierarchy}

\begin{theorem}[Imported, corrected form; \cite{Work} v2]\label{thm:law}
Under battery-assisted thermal operations at temperature $T$ with a Markovian
thermal-fixed-point generator:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] (unconditional) $\Pmin(\rho) \ge \kB T\,\sigma(\rho)$, with
$\sigma \ge 0$ the entropy production rate, split as $\sigma =
\dot{F}^\Delta_{\mathrm{diag}} + \dot{C}_{\Delta,\mathrm{loss}}$;
\item[(b)] (coherence power) $\Pmin(\rho) \ge \kB
T\,\dot{C}_{\Delta,\mathrm{loss}}(\rho)$ under diagonal contraction
(automatic for Davies generators);
\item[(c)] (extra power) against thermodynamically efficient
population-maintenance baselines, and assumption-free in the free-baseline
(passive) case, the incremental power obeys $\Pextra(\rho) \ge \kB
T\,\dot{C}_{\Delta,\mathrm{loss}}(\rho)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Correction of v1's citation pointer]\label{rem:pointer}
v1 imported this as ``$\Pextra \ge \kB T\dot{C}_{\Delta,\mathrm{loss}}$'' from
[6, Theorem 4.12], defining $\Pextra$ as an infimum over \emph{unlinked}
pairs of strategies. That definition is vacuous (the infimum is $-\infty$;
battery-burning baselines), and \cite{Work} v1's Theorem~4.12 was corrected
accordingly. Theorem~\ref{thm:law} is the corrected hierarchy; the pipeline
uses (a)--(c) in the appropriate regime.
\end{remark}

\begin{assumption}[$E$-track extension (optional)]\label{ass:etrack}
For general $E$, assume a sufficiency/Pythagorean identity strong enough to
upgrade Theorem~\ref{thm:law} to $\Pmin(\rho) \ge \kB
T\,\dot{C}_{E,\mathrm{loss}}(\rho)$; all $E$-track power statements are
conditional on this.
\end{assumption}

\section{Static geometry as suppressibility}

\begin{definition}[Static leakage proxy]
$\mathrm{Leak}(\eps)$ is any nonnegative measure of cross-interface
dependence at effective width $\eps$ (a correlation norm, a
weak-correlation mutual-information proxy, or a recoverability-relevant
cross-correlation).
\end{definition}

In massive homogeneous models the canonical envelope is $\mathrm{Leak}(\eps)
\lesssim \mathrm{poly}(m\eps)\, K_\nu(m\eps) \sim \mathrm{poly}(m\eps)
e^{-m\eps}$; the lattice/continuum instances (with the Combes--Thomas rate
$\mathrm{arccosh}(1+m^2/2)$ verified in the companion) are developed in
\cite{Clustering,NIST}.

\section{Recoverability layer via conditional mutual information}

\begin{lemma}[Elementary fidelity bound]\label{lem:fid}
For all $F \in (0,1]$, $\;1 - F \le -\log F$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
$g(F) := -\log F - (1-F)$ has $g(1) = 0$ and $g'(F) = (F-1)/F \le 0$ on
$(0,1]$, so $g \ge 0$. (Verified numerically to the double-precision floor,
Appendix~\ref{app:verif}.)
\end{proof}

For a tripartition $A$ (protected interior), $B$ (collar/membrane), $C$
(exterior), the Fawzi--Renner theorem \cite{FawziRenner15} gives a recovery
map $\mathcal{R}_B$ with $-\log F(\rho_{ABC}, \mathcal{R}_B(\rho_{AB})) \le
\tfrac12 I(A:C|B)$, hence by Lemma~\ref{lem:fid} a \emph{recoverability proxy}
$1 - F \le \tfrac12 I(A:C|B)$. Clustering across the collar bounds
$I(A:C|B)$ by a leakage envelope, giving leakage $\to$ reconstruction. This
layer uses only CMI and Fawzi--Renner and is \emph{independent} of the
Petz/reattachment distinction of \cite{Clustering} (v2); the quantitative
Gaussian reconstruction-fidelity bound of \cite{Clustering} is the sharper,
model-specific companion of this soft CMI statement.

\section{A minimal microscopic route to RIP-U (Davies interface lemma)}
\label{sec:davies}

\begin{lemma}[Correlator envelope $\Rightarrow$ Davies upper-rate envelope]
\label{lem:davies}
In a validated Davies weak-coupling--secular regime \cite{Davies74,BreuerPetruccione},
let the coupling's Bohr components $S(\omega)$ have spatial tails beyond the
interface obeying $\|\mathrm{tail}_\eps(S(\omega))\|_\infty \le
A(1+\eps)^p e^{-\eps/\xi}$, with bounded weighted rates. Then the effective \emph{upper} rate envelope on observables at distance
$\eps$ satisfies the \emph{linear} bound
\begin{equation}
\kup(\eps) \;\le\; C\,(1+\eps)^p\, e^{-\eps/\xi}.
\end{equation}
This is the upper-envelope (RIP-U) direction and coincides with the
\emph{linear} envelope lemma of \cite{DaviesCore} (v2).
\end{lemma}

\begin{remark}[Linear vs.\ squared envelopes]\label{rem:linsq}
The generally justified Davies (correlator-mediated) statement is the
\emph{linear} envelope above. A \emph{squared} envelope
$e^{-2\eps/\xi}$ belongs to amplitude-mediated local-sink / quasi-free
settings, where it is exact with a frequency-resolved exponent
\cite{HCut} (v2); it must \emph{not} be read as a general Davies
consequence. This is exactly the distinction \cite{DaviesCore} (v2) had to
draw when its v1 quadratic Bohr-block envelope --- which relied on a
Dirichlet identity false for $\omega \neq 0$ --- was withdrawn as proved and
replaced by the linear form.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Direction and the exact Bohr-block identity]
Only the \emph{upper}-envelope (RIP-U) direction follows from a tail bound;
lower envelopes (floors) require a separate witness (\S8). The Dirichlet form
underlying $\kup$ is the exact modular-weighted Bohr-block form of
\cite{DaviesCore} (v2), \emph{not} the commutator form; at $\omega = 0$ it
reduces to $\tfrac{\gamma(0)}{2}\|[S(0),O]\|^2_{2,\sigma}$, and the
$\omega = 0$ witness rate is verified to decrease with separation
(Appendix~\ref{app:verif}). The near-zero-frequency channel is precisely
where floors arise \cite{Stress} (v2).
\end{remark}

\section{The dynamical hinge: upper and lower envelopes}

\begin{definition}[Ceiling and floor envelopes]
On a specified family of observables/states at separation $\eps$, let
$\kup(\eps)$ be the largest effective rate (a supremum/ceiling) and
$\kdn(\eps)$ the smallest nonzero effective rate (an infimum/floor).
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[This distinction was ahead of its time]
v1 already separated $\kup$ from $\kdn$ ``to avoid sign/quantifier errors''.
The companion \cite{Stress} v1 conflated them, inferring a maintenance floor
from a ceiling; its v2 corrected exactly this, adopting the present paper's
distinction. Here the two envelopes drive different conclusions:
\emph{suppression} needs $\kup \to 0$; a \emph{resource horizon} needs
$\kdn$ bounded below.
\end{remark}

\begin{hypothesis}[RIP-U: upper-envelope inheritance]\label{hyp:ripU}
There exist $B > 0$, $\beta \ge 0$ with $\kup(\eps) \le
B(m\eps)^\beta e^{-m\eps}$. (Microscopically supported by
Lemma~\ref{lem:davies}; \emph{proved} in the quasi-free local-sink class,
with the exact frequency-resolved exponent $\kappa(\eps) \propto
e^{-2q(\omega_b)\eps}$, in \cite{HCut} v2.)
\end{hypothesis}

\begin{hypothesis}[RIP-U with floor]\label{hyp:floor}
There exist $\kappa_0 \ge 0$, $B > 0$, $\beta \ge 0$ with
$\kdn(\eps) \ge \kappa_0$ and $\kup(\eps) \le \kappa_0 +
B(m\eps)^\beta e^{-m\eps}$. (The floor $\kappa_0 > 0$ is realized within the
Davies class through near-zero-frequency channels, \cite{Stress} v2.)
\end{hypothesis}

\begin{hypothesis}[RIP-L: lower-envelope inheritance (optional)]\label{hyp:ripL}
A matching lower scaling $\kdn(\eps) \ge b(m\eps)^\beta e^{-m\eps}$, needed
only if one wants power \emph{lower} bounds to \emph{decay} with $\eps$;
posited, not assumed.
\end{hypothesis}

\section{Pipeline theorem}

Let $P^\star_{\mathrm{maint}}$ denote the relevant maintenance-power
quantity: $\Pmin$ in the total-power regime (Theorem~\ref{thm:law}(a)/(b)),
the incremental power against efficient baselines in the extra-power regime,
and $\Pextra = \Pmin$ in the free-baseline case
(Theorem~\ref{thm:law}(c)).

\begin{theorem}[Hypotheses $\Rightarrow$ valid implications]\label{thm:pipeline}
Fix a family $\mathcal{F}_\eps$ and assume:
(P1) the relevant case of the maintenance law Theorem~\ref{thm:law} holds on
$\mathcal{F}_\eps$; (P2) rate proportionality $\Closs(\rho) \ge \kdn(\eps)
C(\rho)$ on $\mathcal{F}_\eps$ (a validated weak-coherence-regime hypothesis).
Then
\begin{equation}
P^\star_{\mathrm{maint}}(\rho) \;\ge\; \kB T\,\kdn(\eps)\,C(\rho)
\qquad (\rho \in \mathcal{F}_\eps).
\end{equation}
Under RIP-U (Hyp.~\ref{hyp:ripU}) the rate-induced \emph{lower-bound
obstruction} to maintenance decays with $\eps$; if, in addition, an
achievability / efficient-control \emph{upper} bound matches the rate
envelope, then a fixed budget eventually suffices at large $\eps$ (cheap
isolation). Under a floor (Hyp.~\ref{hyp:floor}) with $\kappa_0 > 0$,
$P^\star_{\mathrm{maint}} \ge \kB T\kappa_0 C_0$ for all $\eps$ --- a
resource horizon that no separation removes.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Quantifier hygiene]
The two conclusions use \emph{different} envelopes: cheap isolation is a
statement about $\kup$ (some strategy becomes affordable), the horizon a
statement about $\kdn$ (no strategy escapes the floor). Conflating them is the
error \cite{Stress} v2 fixed; keeping them separate is this paper's
organizing discipline.
\end{remark}

\section{Resource horizon and a membrane testbed}

If $\kdn(\eps) \ge \kappa_0 > 0$ then, by Theorem~\ref{thm:pipeline},
sustaining organization above $C_0$ costs at least $\kB T\kappa_0 C_0$ at any
separation. \textbf{Proposed testbed:} membrane-embedded spin probes with a
tunable dissipative site; measure the dephasing-rate envelope vs.\ interface
width, engineer a DC (near-zero-frequency) leak, and verify a shift in the
inferred floor --- an operational detection of a resource horizon. Rate
extraction must follow the proxy-validation discipline of the series
(fixed-absolute-time fits, gap-removed/critical control, baseline
subtraction; \cite{HCut,Stress} v2), never co-moving windows.

\section{Conclusion}

The program composes a proved maintenance law, a static geometric suppression
layer, a CMI recoverability layer, a Davies interface route to RIP-U, and a
ceiling/floor hinge into an end-to-end operational pipeline from geometry to
maintenance cost --- with ``life'' as sustained organization maintenance under
finite budgets, an operational reading only. The paper's early separation of
$\kup$ from $\kdn$ has been vindicated by the corrected companion series; its
one genuinely original technical contribution, the CMI recoverability layer
and the Davies upper-envelope lemma, is verified where checkable.

\section{Verification of the checkable claims}\label{app:verif}

Distributed as \texttt{verification/0085/verify\_0085.py}. (A)
Lemma~\ref{lem:fid}: $\min_{F\in(0,1]}[-\log F - (1-F)] = 0$ to the
double-precision floor. (B) Davies upper-envelope direction
(Lemma~\ref{lem:davies}, RIP-U): for TFIM $N=8$, $\beta=1$, $h/J=1.5$, the
exact $\omega=0$ witness rate $R_{\mathrm{opt}}(\eps)$ decreases monotonically
with separation ($0.1346 \to 0.0886 \to 0.0684$ at $\eps = 1,2,3$),
confirming that a decaying coupling tail yields a decaying upper-rate
envelope. Floors ($\kdn > 0$) require the separate near-zero-frequency
mechanism of \cite{Stress} v2 and are not produced by this local-coupling
model.
